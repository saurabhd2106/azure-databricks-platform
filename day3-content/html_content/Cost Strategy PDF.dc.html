05 · OPTIMISATION LEVERS · 4.1 COMPUTE

Compute: the largest lever

Match compute to workloadProduction pipelines on Jobs Compute, not All-Purpose. All-Purpose for interactive development; SQL Warehouses or serverless for BI.~½ DBU rate
Autoscaling & auto-terminationEnable both on every cluster; interactive clusters stop after 15–30 minutes idle. Cluster pools for frequently starting jobs.15–30 min
Spot VMsSpot workers on fault-tolerant batch jobs; driver on-demand, with automatic fallback to on-demand workers.60–80% VM
Right-sizingResize under-used clusters from utilisation metrics. Memory-optimised for joins and shuffles; compute-optimised for ETL-heavy stages.
PhotonHigher DBU rate per hour, but SQL and Delta-heavy jobs often finish 2–3x faster, so net cheaper. Benchmark first.2–3x faster
Shared job clustersIn multi-task workflows, share one job cluster across tasks to avoid repeated startup cost.
SQL Warehouse sizingServerless warehouses with 5–10 minute auto-stop, right-sized T-shirt sizes and a capped maximum number of clusters.5–10 min

05 · OPTIMISATION LEVERS · 4.2–4.4

Data, network and commercial terms

4.2 Data & Storage
Delta table hygienePredictive optimisation or scheduled OPTIMIZE; liquid clustering over deep static partitioning; VACUUM on a sensible retention schedule.
ADLS lifecycleBronze and raw data to Cool or Archive on a schedule; auto-delete stale checkpoints and temporary job output.
Incremental processingAuto Loader and incremental patterns (or Delta Live Tables) instead of full reloads; MERGE rather than overwrite.
4.3 Network
Same regionWorkspace, storage account and downstream consumers in one Azure region to avoid cross-region transfer charges.
NAT Gateway & egressMonitor specifically on VNet-injected workspaces; easy to overlook, grows quickly with high-throughput jobs.
Private EndpointsUse deliberately, not by default, weighing security benefit against added networking cost.
4.4 Commercial terms
DBCUPre-purchase DBUs through Azure on a 1- or 3-year term at a discount, once usage is stable enough to size accurately.
Reserved VMs / Savings PlanCover the always-on baseline: steady-state jobs and always-on SQL Warehouses.
MACC & Hybrid BenefitMarketplace Databricks spend counts toward a Microsoft consumption commitment; Azure Hybrid Benefit cuts licensing on eligible VMs.
