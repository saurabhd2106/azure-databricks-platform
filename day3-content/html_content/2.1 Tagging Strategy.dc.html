Three workloads, three compute types, one cost owner

Each compute type takes tags in a different place. The strategy has to cover all three, or spend leaks out of the chargeback report.

T2 · TAG TAXONOMY

Four mandatory keys, controlled values

Keys are case-sensitive and must be spelled identically everywhere, or Azure and Databricks reports will split one team into several. Values come from a controlled list, not free text.
